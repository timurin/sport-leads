## 21.1.2 Domain contract (closed) — `SL-USERS-CABINET-v1`

### Two identities (no merge)

| Entity | Owns | Does not own |
|--------|------|--------------|
| **PlatformUser** (this Stage) | Login, password hash, display name, activity, roles/permissions, invite/pending access to ERP | Org HR job title, payroll, org chart as SoT |
| **Employee** (`2.4.2`) | Org HR directory: contacts, department, position inside company structure | ERP login / roles |

Soft link later (optional FK `platform_user_id` on Employee or reverse) — **out of `21.*` MVP** unless a microtask adds it. UI may show “связанный сотрудник” as future field.

### Access (extends ADR-024, no deny-by-default change)

- List/cabinet gated by `admin.roles.assign` for **role mutation** (as today).
- Broader “view users list” may reuse same permission in MVP (`21.2`/`21.4`); finer `admin.users.read` — optional later ADR amend.
- `21.4`: UI shows roles **and** readable permission/module matrix (derived from role→permission), still assign via role codes; do not invent per-user permission overrides in MVP.
